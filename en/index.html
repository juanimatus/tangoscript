Chemical Group · Rodeo Semillas and Chemical Sud

From answering every question by hand to a catalog that replies on its own

A smart workflow in Make, connected to WhatsApp, that finds the product even when it’s misspelled and sends the purchase link from the store or Mercado Libre.

1,700+messages handled since September 2026
2sales channels connected: store and Mercado Libre
24/7support on WhatsApp, after hours too
See the full case
Situation
The company gets questions about seeds and fertilizers on WhatsApp, and sells through its AgroVerde store, Mercado Libre and distributors.
Challenge
Reply fast without a person answering every message, send each request to the right place to buy, and never link the wrong product.
What I did
I built a Make workflow on WhatsApp Business with a custom search engine: it handles typos, tells apart varieties, sizes and bulk boxes, and matches the store against Mercado Libre. When someone asks for a distributor, it finds one by province and town from a spreadsheet the client keeps updated.
Result
Over 1,700 messages handled without anyone replying by hand. When in doubt, the workflow points to the store rather than to the wrong product.
Crop trials · AppSheet, Make and AI · 2026/27 season

From loose photos on a phone to an archive that sorts itself

An AppSheet app with AI: the technician takes the photo, and AI reads the label, recognizes the variety and files it in Drive.

0photos to rename by hand
13+varieties tracked this season
AIreads the label, even when handwritten
See the full case
Situation
Field trials produce many photos per variety and per day, and they piled up mixed on the phone.
Challenge
Get every photo identified, described and filed in the right place with no extra work in the field.
What I did
I built the app in AppSheet and the automation in Make. GPT-4o reads the photo: it transcribes the label, describes the sample and picks the variety from the catalog. The photo is copied to Drive into its variety’s folder, under that day’s subfolder, named with code and date. If the AI isn’t sure, the entry is flagged for manual review.
Result
The trial log stays organized and described, with no renaming or moving files, and a person only steps in when the AI is unsure.

Common questions.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You know how your business works and I handle the technical side. I explain everything plainly and document how each automation works.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After our first call and the process review, I send you a fixed quote with no hidden costs.

Can you guarantee I’ll save time?

Before we start, we agree on how to measure success. If the review shows automation isn’t worth it for you, I’ll tell you.

Make or n8n?

Make is great for getting started fast with a visual builder. n8n makes sense for high volume or when you need more control and predictable costs. We choose based on your case.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I work with clients in any country, in English or Spanish, over video calls and WhatsApp.

What if something stops working?

Workflows are monitored and documented. If a tool changes or something breaks, I review and fix it. We can also agree on monthly maintenance.
